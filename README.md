🍽️ Zomato Restaurant Analytics & Performance Dashboard

1. Recommended Structure & Headline

Zomato Global Restaurant Intelligence: Exploratory Data Analysis, Cuisine Trends, Delivery vs. Dine-in Impact, and Customer Rating Benchmarks.

2. Short Description & Purpose

Overview
The Zomato Analytics Dashboard is an end-to-end business intelligence solution built in Microsoft Power BI to analyze restaurant operations, dining preferences, pricing trends, and customer sentiment across global metropolitan markets.
By aggregating restaurant-level data points—including cuisine varieties, average dining cost, online ordering availability, and user review scores—this dashboard equips restaurateurs, food aggregators, and hospitality investors with actionable operational insights.

##Core Objectives
●	Market Opportunity Mapping: Identify high-demand cuisine clusters and underserved culinary niches across cities and localities.
●	Service Channel Evaluation: Quantify how enabling Online Delivery and Table Booking impacts aggregate ratings and foot traffic.
●	Pricing & Affordability Indexing: Analyze spending tiers (Average Cost for Two) across different geographical zones.
●	Customer Sentiment Benchmarking: Track the relationship between total vote volume, rating text tiers (Excellent, Very Good, Average, Poor), and overall customer satisfaction.

3. Tech Stack
List the key technologies used to build the dashboard.

Example: The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:
• 📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization platform used for report creation.
• 📂 Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing the data.
• 🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for calculated measures, dynamic visuals, and conditional logic.
• 📝 Data Modeling – Relationships established among tables (resorts, snow, and data_dictionary) to enable cross-filtering and aggregation.
• 📁 File Format – .pbix for development and .png for dashboard previews.

4. Features & Key Highlights
   
1. Executive Summary & KPIs
●	High-Level Cards: Live tallies for Total Restaurants, Distinct Cuisines, Cities Covered, Average Customer Rating, and Cumulative Votes.
●	Quick Filter Slicers: Dynamic single/multi-select filtering by Country, City, Price Range, Online Ordering availability, and Table Reservation status.
2. Service Channel & Capability Matrix
●	Delivery vs. Dine-in Comparative Analysis: Direct side-by-side comparison illustrating rating differentials between outlets offering online delivery vs. dine-in-only establishments.
●	Table Booking Penetration: Evaluation of table reservation features across premium price tiers and luxury dining spots.
3. Cuisine Popularity & Market Density
●	Top 10 Cuisines Leaderboard: Bar chart breakdown tracking dominant offerings (e.g., North Indian, Chinese, Continental, Italian, Fast Food).
●	Locality Cuisine Concentration: Tree-map visualization pinpointing culinary hotspots and regional specialties within selected cities.
4. Cost for Two & Price Tier Distribution
●	Affordability Distribution: Histogram and box-whisker views of dining budgets across price tiers (Budget, Moderate, Fine Dining).
●	City Cost Benchmarking: Average cost comparisons normalized across metropolitan zones.
5. Customer Rating & Sentiment Breakdown
●	Vote Volume vs. Rating Correlation: Scatter plot mapping total review votes against aggregate scores to reveal high-volume consumer favorites vs. niche high-rated gems.
●	Rating Tier Distribution: Donut chart illustrating the breakdown between Excellent, Good, Average, and Poor ratings.
